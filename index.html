What is 99.99%?+

A system-design tycoon where you act as the technical lead of a growing software company. Design its infrastructure, balance cost and capacity, and respond when traffic or failures put your decisions to the test.

Do I need to know system design?+

No production experience is required. The game is designed for computing students with basic web-development knowledge who enjoy strategy games. You'll explore how applications, databases, caching, and scaling work together through their consequences.

How do I play?+

Open the prototype in your browser and press Play. A laptop or desktop is recommended for the architecture view. There's nothing to install, and the early prototype is free to try.

Is this the finished game?+

It's an early playable prototype. We're testing the core loop and improving it with player feedback. The concept mockup and interactive example on this page illustrate the direction; features and balance may change.

How can I help with the playtest?+

Try a run and notice which decisions feel interesting, which controls feel confusing, and whether you'd want to play again. If you're joining a session with the team, share those observations with your facilitator. Your feedback helps shape the next build.
