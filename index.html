One company.
Five chapters.

Your servers, investments and progress carry forward from one chapter to the next.

  1. 1

    Opening

    Traffic grows and the service slows down. Read the latency, errors and backlog, inspect each component and find your first bottleneck.

  2. 2

    Scaling & Routing

    Add servers, put a load balancer in front of them and make sure new capacity actually receives traffic.

  3. 3

    Data Strategy

    Add a read cache, tune it and see how read-heavy and write-heavy workloads change the load on your database.

  4. 4

    Traffic Spikes & Autoscaling

    Demand surges in sudden spikes. Turn on autoscaling and watch it add servers as traffic rises and retire them when it falls.

  5. 5

    Stay Online

    Applications fail. Prepare health checks, spare instances and automatic failover so the service keeps running.

Before you play

Do I need production experience?+

No. Basic web-development knowledge is enough. You learn how scaling, caching, databases and failover work together by seeing the consequences of your choices.

How do I play?+

Open the game in your browser and choose Campaign or Classic. A laptop or desktop works best. There's nothing to install.

Do I need an account?+

No. You can play as a guest, and your progress stays in that browser. Sign in with Google from the title screen to save your company to your account.

Is this the finished game?+

Not yet. CS3216 Group 5 is building it and adding to it every week. Use the Give feedback button in the game to tell us what to improve.
